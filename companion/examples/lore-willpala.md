# The Lore of Willpala's Journey

*A compendium of what **Willpala**, Level 13 Undead Paladin of Classic Beta PvE, has encountered.*  
*Every entry below is the in-game text the game itself showed and the Azeroth Chronicle addon recorded. Nothing here is invented — these are WoW: Forever custom lands with no outside canon.*

**Coverage:** 44 of 56 quests have captured lore text; 12 do not (revisit them in-game to record).

## Lands walked — The Barrens, Stonetalon Mountains, Mulgore, Thunder Bluff, Zephras Isle
