# The Chronicle of Willpala

*An Undead Paladin's road through Zephras Isle — Classic Beta PvE.*
*Every place, quest, and level below is drawn from the recorded journey. Only the telling is embellished.*

---

## Chapter One — The Long Friday

It began at The Crossroads, a level-seven paladin with more road ahead than behind.

Willpala pushed north through Honor's Stand and Greatwood Vale, then up into the thin air of Malaka'jin and the Skywatcher Plateau, where Camp Gev'rek clung to the rock. The first real task came out of Shen'dar — **Hippogryph Harassment**: a flock had nested southeast of Shen'dar Village and were tearing into travelers on the road. The youths, the protectors, and their matriarch were put down, and the road went quiet.

From there the work only multiplied. **Among the Faithful**, **Havoc in the Highlands**, **The Western Watch** — the Shen'dar Highlands and Village gave up quest after quest. A summons, **To Valanaar**, drew the paladin down through the Valanaar Skydocks into Valanaar proper, signet delivered, audience kept with **The Grand Skyseer**. Between errands the map filled in: Windsong Standing Stones, a cleared Bandit Hideout, Falaath Village, the shore of Windsong Lake, the West Pylon Watchtower.

By the time the night wore out, twenty-two quests had been turned in and the paladin stood at level nine — blade still wet, a little further from the grave than when the day started.

## Chapter Two — The Saturday Marathon

The second day opened at level ten, in the Gustberry Lowlands, and it did not slow down.

Willpala cut a loop through the Shriekling Den and Windfield Orchard, back to Valanaar and up to the Shrine of Akir, then into the dark under Shadowgale Forest and the Nightclaw Cavern. The quests came in chains now, half a dozen taken at once and cleared in a sweep: **Make Yourself Useful**, **The Hills Have Eyes**, **Standing Our Ground**, and the sorrowful trio out of the forest — **Tears of the Lady**, **Feathers for Binding**, **Unwanted and Unworthy** — followed by **The Ties That Bind**, **The Wounds of Betrayal**, and a **Ripped Missive** that led to **The Fate of the Den**.

Then the war-work: **Al'Aketh Assassins**, **Blood for Blood**, **Tower Defense** and **Breaking the Breaker** at the East Pylon Watchtower, **Disrupting Logistics**, and a **Return to Valanaar** to answer **The High Elder's Request**. The last thread taken up before the session closed was **The Turncoat** — left hanging, unfinished, for another night.

Thirty-three quests fell that day. The paladin climbed from ten to thirteen without looking up.
